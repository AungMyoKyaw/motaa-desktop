## Advertised output details checked on 18 November 2026

Android promotional descriptions in `paidversion.java` identify two outputs that were missing from their dedicated desktop views. Page 24 now includes ecliptic latitude for each displayed body. Page 28 now includes local Moon-sign, tithi and nakshatra transition times for the selected day. Both views use the existing free Advanced workspace. Dashes denote unavailable calculations.

The electional timing table gives yoga and karana independent transition rows. The ephemeris calendar includes month and year selection in the design and identifies the advertised 3,000-year target. Release bounds depend on verified engine coverage. The picker and month navigation must reject dates beyond those inclusive bounds, keep the current valid selection, and show the supported dates. The Yoga analysis comparison window and all three candidate times follow the shared birth-details toggle and default to hidden.

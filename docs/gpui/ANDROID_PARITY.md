## Source checkpoints

The sibling checkout `../motaa-app` contains the Android evidence used for this audit. Its original package remains separate from the desktop app.

- `src/motaa/motaa/myanmar/UserDBActivity.java` reads the current profile from `MY_TABLE` in column order and loads saved profiles from `commments.db`. `src/motaa/L0/DialogInterfaceOnClickListenerC0030c0.java` serializes saved records, and `k0.java` confirms the exact `Porphyrius` identifier.
- `src/motaa/L0/u0.java` implements the baseline for the scrubber and the six step units.
- `src/motaa/L0/s0.java` sets the MUNP flag. `src/motaa/motaa/myanmar/main.java` switches between `f0` and `i0` in chart rendering. The expanded path adds the three outer planets from the chosen planet epoch and the Midheaven from the chosen Ascendant epoch.
- `main.java` mode 6 calls `p0`. It builds the directed body matrix with `H` and the house-midpoint columns with `G`. The existing Java differential fixtures cover these angle-code helpers, independently of calculated planetary positions.
- `main.java` mode 7 calls `o0`. The mode-7 branch of `S0` fills `O2` from the birth record and `L2` from the current reference. The renderer places `O2` in the inner chart and `L2` in the outer band.
- `main.java` selects the 27- or 28-mansion Jamma classifications with `X0` and `Y0` in the Rahu renderer. The desktop wheel now draws the ten Rahu zones, Sun groups from L0/M0, and the six Jamma classifications from X0/Y0. The renderer uses the original 108-spoke grid in both settings. Verify same-input labels and geometry visually.
- `IZARTARPRO_REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md` documents a sibling iOS product with a separate bundle and incomplete source recovery. Its metadata alone cannot validate the two unfinished premium calculations.
